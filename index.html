    Aftercare

    This is a beach town

    Keep it clean. Keep it out of the Pacific, the pool, and the sun until it is healed. Salt water, chlorine, and a San Clemente afternoon will wreck a fresh tattoo.

    1. Leave the bandage on for the time your artist told you.
    2. Wash with mild unscented soap and lukewarm water. Pat dry. Do not scrub.
    3. A thin layer of whatever ointment they handed you. Not a coat of grease.
    4. No ocean, no pool, no hot tub, no soaking until healed.
    5. No direct sun. Cover it or stay in the shade.
    6. No picking. No scratching.
    7. Spreading redness, heat, pus, fever: call the shop and see a doctor.

    General guidance only — follow what your artist tells you at the chair.
