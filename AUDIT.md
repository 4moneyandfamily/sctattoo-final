## 2. Failures that cost walk-ins and deposits

**Conversion**

- Homepage phone number is missing. The number only appears on Contact and Links.
- No `tel:` or `maps` CTA in the header.
- No booking form. Contact is “LEAVE A MESSAGE” with name / email / phone / message — not idea, artist, size, placement, cover-up, 18+, or a reference photo.
- Deposit is mentioned only on Links, with no amount and no way to pay it.
- Hours on the homepage are “noon- 7-8pm.” Listings say 12–8. MapQuest says 12–7. A visitor cannot tell if they can come now.

**Trust and freshness**

- Portfolio images on the OPP galleries date to the late 2010s (brief: newest around 2019). Instagram is the live portfolio; the website is a graveyard.
- Crew on Links: B.G. @brothergreg, Brian Taylor @intothegave, Chas @cebyassee, Thad @thadart, John Ondo @horishit, James @jameswhelan. The portfolio nav only surfaces Thad, John, Brian, and “Art.” Greg, Chas, and James are easy to miss.
- No aftercare. In a beach town that is a liability and a missed “I just got tattooed, what now?” search.
- No FAQ. California’s 18+ rule with **no parental exception** is not stated. First-timers and parents will bounce to a shop that says it.

**NAP and SEO**

- Contact page prints **“117 Avenida Granda”** — a misspelling of Granada. Same page also prints “117 avenida granada San Clemente, ca.92672.” Google wants one string everywhere.
- No TattooParlor / LocalBusiness schema.
- No sitemap, no Open Graph image that looks like the shop.
- Title is just “San Clemente Tattoo.” No city, no “walk-ins,” no “traditional.”
- Email is still `brogreg777@yahoo.com`. Fine for now; swap to `info@sanclementetattoo.com` before launch (CONFIRM mailbox).

**Design**

- Light gray OPP chrome, centered serif title, one giant image. It does not read as a street shop. It reads as a 2012 artist template.
- The one good asset — the flash painting on the homepage — is asked to carry the entire brand. There is no painted sign, no banner lettering, no shop voice beyond three sentences.

**Accessibility and performance**

- Template is desktop-first. Mobile menu exists, but tap targets and the real jobs (call, go, book) are not designed.
- Images are not described as “artist + style.”
- No reduced-motion, no skip link, no visible focus system worth mentioning.

## 5. What the 2026 benchmark sites actually do (structure, not look)

CyberOptik’s 2026 list (Kings Avenue, Bang Bang, Guns N Needles, Idle Hands, Slave to the Needle, Golden Rule, etc.) repeats the same machine:

1. Sticky identity + one obvious next action (book / walk-in / call).
2. Gallery first, filtered by artist and style.
3. Artist cards that deep-link into that filter.
4. Booking that collects the piece, not a blank “message.”
5. Hours and map that match Google.
6. Fast on a phone.

They do **not** share a look. Copying Bang Bang’s black luxury or Kings Avenue’s polished roster would make this shop look like it wants to be someone else. Structure only.

## 6. Rebuild priorities (what this repo ships)

1. **One-tap bar on every screen:** Call · Directions · Book.
2. **Live open/closed** in `America/Los_Angeles` from the hours in `shop-config.json`.
3. **Gallery** that filters by artist and style, masonry, lightbox, hash deep links. v1 uses labeled sample flash plates; production swaps in Instagram.
4. **Crew cards** that pre-filter the gallery. Handles match the current Links page. Active roster is flagged CONFIRM.
5. **Three-step request:** idea + artist → size + placement + cover-up → contact + 18+ + optional photo.
6. **NAP locked:** 117 Avenida Granada, San Clemente, CA 92672 · (949) 498-8487. Schema included.
7. **Aftercare written for the beach.** FAQ with the California 18+ rule.
8. **Painted shop sign in the hero.** Everything else stays quiet so the work can talk.

Hours, deposit amount, deposit method, individual bios, who is still on the wall, and the launch email are **CONFIRM** in `shop-config.json`. Nothing in this build invents them.
